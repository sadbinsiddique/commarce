## Data Access Object (DIO)

### Methods

1. save() - to save an entity to the database.
2. findById() - to retrieve an entity by its unique identifier.
3. findAll() - to retrieve all entities of a particular type.
4. findByProperty() - to retrieve entities based on specific property values.
5. findByCriteria() - to retrieve entities based on complex criteria or conditions.
6. findByLastName() - to retrieve entities based on their last name.
7. findByEmail() - to retrieve entities based on their email address.
8. update() - to update an existing entity in the database.
9. delete() - to remove an entity from the database.
10. count() - to count the number of entities in the database.
11. existsById() - to check if an entity exists in the database based on its unique identifier.
12. findByPage() - to retrieve a subset of entities based on pagination parameters.
13. findByExample() - to retrieve entities that match the properties of a given example entity
14. findByCustomQuery() - to retrieve entities based on a custom query defined by the developer.
15. findByDateRange() - to retrieve entities based on a specific date range.
16. findByStatus() - to retrieve entities based on their status or state.
17. findByCategory() - to retrieve entities based on their category or type.
18. findByUser() - to retrieve entities based on the user associated with them.
19. findByRole() - to retrieve entities based on the role associated with them.
20. findByPermission() - to retrieve entities based on the permissions associated with them.
21. findByLocation() - to retrieve entities based on their location or geographical area.
22. findByPriceRange() - to retrieve entities based on a specific price range.
23. findByRating() - to retrieve entities based on their rating or review score.
24. findByKeyword() - to retrieve entities based on a specific keyword or search term.
25. findByTag() - to retrieve entities based on specific tags or labels associated with them
26. findByRelationship() - to retrieve entities based on their relationships with other entities.
27. findByHierarchy() - to retrieve entities based on their position in a hierarchical structure.
28. findByAssociation() - to retrieve entities based on their associations with other entities.
29. findByAttribute() - to retrieve entities based on specific attributes or characteristics.
30. deleteById() - to delete an entity from the database based on its unique identifier.
31. deleteByProperty() - to delete entities based on specific property values.
32. deleteByCriteria() - to delete entities based on complex criteria or conditions.
33. deleteByLastName() - to delete entities based on their last name.
34. deleteAll() - to delete all entities of a particular type from the database.
